Try Lafiya in your browser

This is a browser preview, not the glasses. The lens on the left runs the same app code as the glasses, and the chat on the right runs the same bot as the real Telegram group. You get your own practice team. Nobody else sees it, and it's gone after 30 minutes.

Volunteer: what Alex sees on the glasses

  1. Press Tap to check in. The status board in the chat updates.
  2. Type what you would say, then press Say it. Try "gate on Mill Lane is locked".
  3. In the chat, press a reply button under your clue. It shows on the lens. Tap to say "got it".
  4. In the chat, send /ask Alex is the road open?, then pick an answer on the lens.
  5. Send a photo from the chat side. It shows on the lens in grey, the way the glasses draw it.
  6. Send /voice on, tick "Use my microphone", hold to talk, and play the voice note that lands under your clue.
  7. Stop checking in. After about two and a half minutes the coordinator gets a missed check-in notice.
